Message Check

Upload a dating chat and get an honest read on how the two of you communicate.

Your conversation

Drop screenshots here or paste from your clipboard, or click to browse. PNG, JPG, WEBP, GIF, PDF or TXT, up to 10 files.

    Files are sent to Claude for analysis and aren't stored by this app. Only upload chats you're comfortable sharing.

    What you'll get

    Each file is read for the messages inside it, so you can check what was picked up. Then you'll see:

    • Warnings about the communication, from either side, each tied to an exact quote.
    • A compatibility rating with a breakdown and a confidence level.
    • Specific things you could say or do next.

    The rating reflects only the messages you share. It isn't a prediction about the relationship.
